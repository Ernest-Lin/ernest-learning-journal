# AI Exposure and Capital Flows: The Role of Investor Attention after the ChatGPT Shock

This repository contains the replication materials for the empirical finance thesis:

**AI Exposure and Capital Flows: The Role of Investor Attention after the ChatGPT Shock**

The project studies whether the public release of ChatGPT in November 2022 affected U.S. mutual fund capital flows and portfolio allocation. The empirical design uses pre-shock AI exposure as treatment intensity in a Difference-in-Differences framework, with additional tests for prior fund performance and portfolio reallocation.

## Main Empirical Tests

- **H1:** Tests whether funds with higher pre-shock AI exposure experienced stronger post-ChatGPT capital inflows.
- **H2:** Tests whether the fund-flow response is stronger among funds with higher prior alpha.
- **H3:** Tests whether AI-exposed funds adjusted portfolio allocation after ChatGPT through AI portfolio share and new AI positions.

## Data Availability

The repository includes code, regression outputs, figures, and non-proprietary input or intermediate files. Some WRDS/CRSP-derived files are intentionally excluded from public distribution because they may be subject to database licensing restrictions. These include large holdings files, fund-to-portfolio mapping files, and monthly CRSP-derived fund panels.

Researchers with WRDS/CRSP access can reproduce the excluded inputs by following the data construction steps described in the notebooks and thesis methodology chapter.
